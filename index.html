Research dossiers and narration scripts for history, true-crime and story-driven podcasts and channels.

What we do

Research dossierOne episode's research, ready to write from: a verified timeline, numbered primary and scholarly sources with links, the key figures, dates and quotes, and the open or disputed points. $40, delivered within 2 days.
Narration scriptA full episode draft told in your format and register (cold open, setup, turning point, payoff), with a numbered source list for every factual claim. $80 per ~1,500 words (about 12-15 minutes of narration), first draft within 3 days of the brief, one revision round included. Longer scripts pro rata.

How it works

  1. Email us an upcoming episode topic or case.
  2. We send a short outline of what the dossier or script would cover, before you commit to anything.
  3. You approve the outline, we do the work and deliver it as PDF, .docx or plain text.
  4. Payment after you approve the delivered work, by card link or bank transfer. Invoices come from our US company, HireThomas, Inc.
